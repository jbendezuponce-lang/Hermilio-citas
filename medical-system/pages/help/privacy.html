Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This is a demo frontend application. No real personal or medical data is collected, transmitted, or stored on any server. All information you see or enter is generated for demonstration purposes and persists only in your browser's local storage.

Data would be used only to facilitate appointments, maintain medical records, and improve platform functionality — never sold to third parties.
